The note on your door never reaches Google.

At Christmas, owners tape new hours to the door. Customers check Google, Apple Maps and AI assistants instead, and walk to a locked door. Connect spots the days that will be wrong, sends the owner one message with the right hours, and puts them on every app at once.

Open the app → Watch a message go out
