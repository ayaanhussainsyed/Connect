Google leaves
the lights on.

On Christmas Eve, Kahvila Aurora closes at 13. Google, Apple Maps and the AI assistants still say open until 18, so the café stays lit on every phone while its door is locked. Connect notices, asks the owner once, and turns the false light off everywhere.

Open the app → Watch a message go out

Every app

One answer travels down the street.

Each app is a lamp that lights up when it picks up the change: Google within the day, Apple Maps on day 2, the AI assistants on day 7. That’s why Connect asks a week ahead. Answer by 17 December, and every app is lit right on Christmas Eve.

How it works

From false light to the right light, in one tap.

  1. 01 · Notice

    {{ floorrow('07:30-18:00', '07:30-13:00') }}

    Connect compares what the apps will show on every coming holiday with what the owner really does, and finds the cold windows.

  2. 02 · Suggest

    {{ floorrow('07:30-13:00') }}
