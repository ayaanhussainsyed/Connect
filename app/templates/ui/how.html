Under the hood

How Connect decides

Five steps for every business, every season. The owner only ever sees step 3, and taps once.

  1. 1
    Detect
    For each special day, work out exactly what Google, Apple Maps and AI assistants will show (100% match with Fonecta's answer key on 22,218 days).
  2. 2
    Suggest
    A machine-learning engine predicts the real hours from the owner's past seasons, linked holidays, the shopping centre, similar businesses nearby and reviews, with a calibrated confidence.
  3. 3
    Contact
    One message per season, on the owner's own channel and language, with a link that needs no login. Silence when the listing is already right.
  4. 4
    Decide
    One tap, a digit, or the owner's own words in Finnish, Swedish, Norwegian or English. Edits are read back before saving.
  5. 5
    Apply
    Written through Yext, read back, and the owner is told when each app will show it.

It only speaks when it's worth it

Every suggestion is scored with Fonecta's own rules: a right fix earns points, a wrong open/closed costs more, a needless message costs a little. Connect sends a suggestion only when it's worth more than staying quiet.

It learns, safely

Answers become memory
The newest answer wins; a "no" is never asked again. Measured: 58.3 → 59.6 points, harmful suggestions 79 → 72.
"Every year" with a safety check
Next season Connect applies the owner's hours itself, only when this year's model agrees, and always with an undo: wrong open/closed drops from 10.1% to 3.5%.
One smart question
For new customers, it asks about the one date where an answer is worth the most: +0.50 points per business vs +0.09 for a random date.
The owner always wins
The listing is re-read before every write; if the owner changed a date themselves, Connect leaves it alone.

Ask Connect: answers that cite their sources

Each business gets its own knowledge graph (its hours, holidays, answers, reviews and similar businesses). Questions are answered only from that graph (Graph RAG); every sentence carries its source, and any date or time not in the facts is rejected.

More than holidays

Weekly hours that changed
Spotted from reviews and Google user edits; every proposed week tested was exactly right (precision 1.00).
"Closed today"
The owner texts it; every app shows it within minutes; the reason is never stored.
Replies to "it was closed" reviews
Drafted from the confirmed hours, sent only with the owner's OK.
Closed for good
Flagged for a Fonecta person instead of messaging a business that may no longer exist.

Every action is in a tamper-evident audit log (Fonecta staff → Verify). All numbers: README, measured on Fonecta's synthetic data with its own scorer.
