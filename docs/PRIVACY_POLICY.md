# Privacy Policy: Connect Suggests (hackathon prototype)

*Last updated: 7 November 2026*

**Connect Suggests** is a prototype built by Team Connect for Fonecta's challenge at the Since AI Hackathon 2026
(Turku, Finland). It shows how a business could receive opening-hours suggestions by WhatsApp or email, and approve them
with one tap. It is a demonstration, not a commercial service.

## What we process
- **Phone numbers and email addresses of test recipients,** people who agreed to receive demo messages from us. All
  business data in the prototype is synthetic: the businesses, their hours and their reviews are invented.
- **Messages:** the demo suggestions we send, and the replies or button taps we receive.

We don't collect location, contacts, payment details or anything else from your device.

## Why
To deliver the demo messages and process your reply: approve, edit, decline or undo a suggested change to a demo
business's opening hours. Nothing else, and never for marketing.

**Legal basis:** your consent as a test participant. You can withdraw it at any time.

## Who else is involved
- **Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform)** delivers WhatsApp messages.
- **Our email provider** delivers emails.
- **A cloud database (MongoDB Atlas)** stores the demo records.

We don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

## How long we keep it
- **Message texts** are deleted automatically after 30 days.
- **Everything else from the demo** is deleted when the hackathon project ends, by 31 December 2026 at the latest.

## Your rights
You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or to stop receiving messages, at any time:
- **WhatsApp:** reply **STOP** to stop receiving messages.
- **Contact us** using the link below and we will delete your data within 7 days.

You can also complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).

## Data deletion
To have your data deleted, contact us using the link below with the phone number or email address you used. We
delete all messages and records linked to it.
